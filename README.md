## Featured Case Study

### An AI-Assisted Case Study on Operational Improvement & Guest Experience

One of the main projects in this portfolio analyses **2,611+ Booking.com reviews across 20+ languages** to identify recurring guest feedback, operational themes and changes over time.

The case study explores:

* What guests repeatedly mention
* Which operational areas generate recurring feedback
* How guest feedback can be structured and analysed
* Changes observed after operational improvements
* How AI can assist with large-scale qualitative analysis
* The limitations of observational before-and-after analysis

The objective is not to claim that a single intervention caused a change in guest ratings, but to demonstrate how **guest feedback can be transformed into operational insight**.
